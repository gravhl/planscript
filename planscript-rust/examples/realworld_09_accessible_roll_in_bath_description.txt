Derived layout description:
The example models an accessible three-quarter bathroom entered from a hall. It includes a wide door, roll-under sink, comfort-height toilet transfer zone, roll-in shower, and an open central turning area.

PlanScript interpretation notes:
- PlanScript does not currently have pocket-door syntax, so the wide entry is represented as a wider swinging door.
- Grab bars and benches are described in labels/notes rather than separate catalog objects.
- The layout intentionally leaves the center of the room clear.
